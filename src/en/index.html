Remember every clue. The deduction is yours.

Mixu is an offline-first mystery reader. It arranges what you have already read into an interactive case board—characters, locations, events, and conflicting testimonies, all linking back to the original text. It never spoils, never guesses the culprit, and never deduces for you.

See how it works (jump to 3-step demo) View client on GitHub (open-source client repo)

Core Values

  1. Shows only what you've read. The case board filters strictly by your reading progress: on Chapter 10, you won't see an identity revealed only in Chapter 14.
  2. One tap back to the original text. Every entry anchors to the source sentence—jump back and forth without losing your place.
  3. Read an entire book without signing in. Imports, reading, bookmarks, notes, and downloaded graph packs are all fully offline. E-Ink devices use the exact same package, just with reduced animations.

Who It Is For

  • Mystery readers tackling complex novels with vast casts and twisted timelines who forget "who was that again?" after a few days.
  • Readers who love taking their own notes and maintaining suspect boards.
  • E-Ink readers who just want to finish a book in peace.

Who It Is Not For (Honest Limitations)

  • Readers wanting "AI to solve the mystery and name the culprit": this is fundamentally against our product principles.
  • Readers seeking online web novel streaming, pirate sources, or DRM circumvention: this is not what Mixu is designed for.
  • Mixu is currently in development. This site describes design goals, not released features.
  • "Spoiler-free" is an engineering objective: deterministic filtering based on reading progress backed by failing regression tests; it is not a mathematical guarantee.
  • Client code is open source (Apache-2.0). Hosted sync services and graph packs are licensed separately. Book titles, brand marks, and third-party literary works are not covered by the open source license.
